# Homepage (Persuade)

Scope: `/` and `/en/` homepage, and the shared visual world every other page inherits from it.
Audience: owner of a small/mid CZ/DE company first, their ops/IT lead second (PRODUCT.md).
Action: start a per-service inquiry (guided, sorted by n8n). Secondary: open a service, see the Kesia case.
Proof: the Kesia case only; the workflow and the inquiry flow themselves demonstrate the competence.
Constraints: deep navy, dark; no invented numbers (saved time/money shown as outputs without figures,
or from `homeNumbers.ts` with its placeholder flags); no hidden interactions; CS + EN; reduced motion = still.
Replaces: the 3D card stack hero (Honza doubted visitors would know the cards are clickable).
Memorable moment: a light packet runs through the services as an automation workflow inside the open file
and arrives at "a running business", throwing off time and money saved as byproducts.
Unresolved: real figures for saved time/money; whether the inquiry's first step sits in the hero or just below.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is ADV's open client file, and inside it the business runs as one automation workflow:
each service is a step the work passes through, ending in a running business. Refuses the split hero with a
product shot and the row of equal service cards.
OWN-WORLD: navy linen bookcloth ground (#060d24 to #13286b), blind-debossed display type, a steel-foil lit
edge (#c9d1de) on every file surface, cool paper index cards (#eef1f6) for anything the visitor fills in,
mono file stamps (FILE 0001, TAB 01), one Ice live-state colour for the moving packet and focus only.
STORY: the visitor sees their messy day turned into a running business by four steps, understands which
service does what, clicks the step that is their problem, and opens a new file (the inquiry) for it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: nav on the cloth; left third the debossed headline, one-line lede, primary "Start an
inquiry" and secondary "See our work"; right two thirds the open file spread with the workflow: input card
("today: manual work, scattered tools") → Business optimisation → Automation & AI → Prototypes & software →
Websites → output card "A running business", with "time back" and "lower costs" branching off as byproduct
slips. Each step node is a labelled tab-card link to its service page. A packet of Ice light travels the
foil connectors on a loop; each node lights and stamps as it passes.
FORM: candidate 5 of 7 (The Client File), steered by Honza with an animated workflow; seed:adce78628a6dcf67.
Raises: numbered step column in the inquiry (origami); one unbroken progress rail with a now-mark (step row);
the hero is one held moment, no carousel (wuxia); named inquiry states draft / filed / sorted / answered
(chromatophore).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
